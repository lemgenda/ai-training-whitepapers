1. Abstract

The Dedicated Image Restoration Suite establishes an authoritative, single-task deep learning framework engineered for physical image restoration across four classical degradation domains: Deblurring and Denoising (NAFNet), Multi-Stage Progressive Deraining (MPRNet), Dual-Residual Exposure and Low-Light Enhancement (MIRNet v2), and Feature Fusion Atmospheric Dehazing (FFANet). Each architecture in Category 04 operates without multi-task cross-talk, maximizing domain-specific reconstruction fidelity, peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR), and structural similarity (SSIM).

2. Physical Optical Degradation Taxonomy

Each dedicated restoration architecture targets a distinct mathematical formulation of optical corruption:

  • Motion & Defocus Blur ($\mathbf{I}_B$): Modeled as a spatially variant blur kernel $\mathbf{K}$ acting on clean latent image $\mathbf{I}$: $$\mathbf{I}_B = \mathbf{I} \otimes \mathbf{K} + \mathbf{N}$$
  • Sensor Noise ($\mathbf{I}_N$): Heteroscedastic additive white Gaussian and Poisson sensor noise: $$\mathbf{I}_N \sim \mathcal{P}(lpha \mathbf{I}) + \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$$
  • Rain Streaks & Veiling Effect ($\mathbf{I}_R$): Additive physical rain layer $\mathbf{R}$ with transmission modulation $\mathbf{T}$: $$\mathbf{I}_R = \mathbf{I} \odot \mathbf{T} + \mathbf{R}$$
  • Atmospheric Haze ($\mathbf{I}_H$): Koschmieder atmospheric scattering model governed by scene depth $d(x)$ and extinction coefficient $eta$: $$\mathbf{I}_H(x) = \mathbf{I}(x) t(x) + A(1 - t(x)), \quad t(x) = e^{-eta d(x)}$$
  • Low-Light & Extreme Exposure ($\mathbf{I}_E$): Non-linear photometric sensor under-illumination with dynamic range clipping.
