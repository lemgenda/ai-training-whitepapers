1. Abstract

The Advanced Restoration and Super-Resolution Master Suite (Category 14) unifies four specialized models: Universal Film Restorer, UltraZoom Super-Resolution, UPN v2 Parameter Predictor, and the Professional Multi-Task Restoration MoE network.

2. Visual Taxonomy: Restoration Manifolds

Processes archival physical film damage, sub-pixel optical downsampling, degradation parameter regression, and compound degradations across four dedicated manifolds.

3. Shared Foundations & Universal Acceleration

Loss optimization combines Charbonnier reconstruction with VGG perceptual supervision:

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}} = \sqrt{\|I_{\text{restored}} - I_{\text{gt}}\|^2 + \epsilon^2} + \lambda_{\text{perceptual}} \|\phi(I_{\text{restored}}) - \phi(I_{\text{gt}})\|_2^2$$

Architecture: Deep Residual Dense Autoencoder.

Status: Checkpoint Trained (LemGendaryUniversalFilmRestorer.pt).

5. Challenges & Resilience Architecture

Task interference across multi-task heads is neutralized using gradient projection and normalized loss scaling, while sub-pixel shuffle layers are initialized to avoid checkerboard artifacts.

6. Deployment Strategy

Engines deploy via TensorRT and ONNX Runtime with FP16 mixed-precision and dynamic batch sizing.
