2. Four-Quadrant Quality Taxonomy

Quality assessment decouples visual aesthetics from technical integrity across four distinct quadrants:

  • Quadrant 1 (High Aesthetic / High Technical): Masterpiece compositions with sharp focus, flawless lighting, and rich artistic impact.
  • Quadrant 2 (High Aesthetic / Low Technical): Poetic compositions marred by optical aberrations, sensor noise, or motion blur.
  • Quadrant 3 (Low Aesthetic / Low Technical): Corrupted images with heavy compression artifacts and deficient composition.
  • Quadrant 4 (Low Aesthetic / High Technical): Razor-sharp, clinically clean images devoid of artistic balance or emotional resonance.
