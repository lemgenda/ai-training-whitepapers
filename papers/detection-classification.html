3. Shared Foundations & Universal Acceleration

Bounding box regression optimizes Complete IoU (CIoU), while categorical classification utilizes Focal Cross-Entropy:

$$\text{CIoU} = \text{IoU} - \left( \frac{\rho^2(b, b^{\text{gt}})}{c^2} + \alpha v \right), \quad \text{FL}(p_t) = -\alpha_t (1 - p_t)^\gamma \log(p_t)$$
