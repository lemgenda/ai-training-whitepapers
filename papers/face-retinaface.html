1. Abstract

RetinaFace in the LemGendary ecosystem is a RetinaFace-style single-box regressor (RetinaFaceMobileNet, models/detection.py in the training suite) for one face box and 5-point landmark regression (left eye, right eye, nose tip, left mouth corner, right mouth corner) per image. A MobileNetV3-Small backbone (mobilenet_v2 optional) feeds one globally pooled feature vector (adaptive average pooling) into three linear heads: one normalized cx cy w h box, one sigmoid face confidence and 10 landmark values (five 2D points). There are no anchors, no feature pyramid (FPN), no deformable convolutions and no ResNet-50 variant, so the model finds one face per image, not several faces or small faces in a crowd. With the MobileNetV3-Small backbone it has 935,663 parameters (counted from the code). A multi-scale anchor head (FPN levels with prior boxes and per-anchor outputs) is an open owner decision. Latency is not yet measured.

1.1 What RetinaFace Does (In Plain English)

Imagine an intelligent autofocus system that finds the main face in a photo and marks its key points:

  • One-Face Localization: RetinaFace looks at the whole image and returns one box for its main face, as in the one-face SFHQ photos it trains on. Crowd scenes with many or tiny faces need the multi-scale anchor head that is still an open owner decision. Its speed is not yet measured.
  • 5-Point Landmark Pinpointing: For that face it marks 5 geometric points: the center of each eye, the tip of the nose, and the two corners of the mouth.
  • Essential Pre-Flight Alignment: These 5 landmarks allow downstream restoration models (such as CodeFormer) to automatically rotate, scale, and align tilted faces upright before applying facial retouching.

2. Visual Taxonomy: Multi-Scale Features & Landmarks

RetinaFace anchors 5 canonical facial landmarks to standardize facial alignment across the LemGendary Face Suite [THEORETICAL]:

  • Point 1 (Left Eye): Anatomical Target: Pupil center / ocular iris; Primary Utility in Pipeline: Geometric tilt calculation & eye restoration guidance
  • Point 2 (Right Eye): Anatomical Target: Pupil center / ocular iris; Primary Utility in Pipeline: Horizontal eye-line leveling & inter-ocular scale reference
  • Point 3 (Nose Tip): Anatomical Target: Pronasale nasal apex; Primary Utility in Pipeline: 3D facial pitch/yaw pose estimation & perspective correction
  • Point 4 (Mouth Left): Anatomical Target: Left oral cheilion / lip corner; Primary Utility in Pipeline: Oral symmetry baseline & mouth crop bounding
  • Point 5 (Mouth Right): Anatomical Target: Right oral cheilion / lip corner; Primary Utility in Pipeline: Lip alignment & expressive deformation monitoring

3. Shared Foundations: Tri-Head Multi-Task Objective

Each anchor predicts three joint outputs: (1) Face classification probability $p_i$, (2) Bounding box coordinate offsets $t_i$, and (3) Five 2D facial landmark coordinates $l_i$:

$$\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{cls}}(p_i, p_i^*) + \lambda_1 p_i^* \text{SmoothL1}(t_i, t_i^*) + \lambda_2 p_i^* \text{SmoothL1}(l_i, l_i^*)$$

The formula above is the published anchor-based objective. The training suite's RetinaFaceMobileNet (v16.9.207) has no prior boxes: one globally pooled head regresses a single normalized cx cy w h box, one confidence and five landmarks per image, so the sum runs over images instead of anchors. The loss weights are confidence 1, box 2 and landmarks 1 (smooth L1 on normalized coordinates); landmarks whose ten values are all zero count as absent and are masked out of the loss.

4. Model Deep-Dives

RetinaFace Multi-Scale Landmark & Localization Engine

4.1 Model Description, Purpose and Usage

Single-stage face localization and 5-point landmark regression engine utilizing the standardized MobileNetV3-Small backbone for high-efficiency edge execution and real-time pre-flight alignment.

4.2 Model Info

  • Architecture: RetinaFace-style single-box regressor (RetinaFaceMobileNet, MobileNetV3-Small backbone, mobilenet_v2 supported; globally pooled box, confidence and landmark heads)
  • Input Resolution: 640x640
  • Parameters: 935,663 with MobileNetV3-Small (927,008 backbone, 8,655 heads; counted from models/detection.py, the registry parameters_m: 27.2 does not match the code)
  • Precision: ONNX FP16 / PyTorch FP32
  • Latency: not measured

4.3 Manifold Info

  • Dataset: LemGendizedRetinaFace
  • Total Samples: 425,223 SFHQ photos (train 374,199 / val 51,024), one face per image; val drawn by SFHQ file id, the same split as LemGendizedParseNet
  • Labels: tight WIDER-style box and five points (eye centres, nose tip, mouth corners) rebuilt from the SFHQ 110-point landmarks (landmark_source: sfhq-110pt), stored as JSON (face_landmarks)
  • Primary Task: 5-point facial landmark regression and face box localization

4.4 Performance Metrics

The training suite measures RetinaFace with four metrics (v16.9.224, training/training/face_detection_metrics.py). The targets are calibrated against standard single-shot face localisation benchmarks:

  • Box IoU (box_iou): mean IoU of the predicted and the labelled box over the images that hold a face. Target $\ge 0.72$ [TARGET].
  • AP50 (ap50): single-class AP over images; each image is one detection ranked by its confidence (used as returned, no second sigmoid) and is a true positive when it holds a face and the IoU reaches 0.5. Target $\ge 0.95$ [TARGET].
  • AP50-95 (ap50_95): the same AP averaged over the COCO thresholds 0.50:0.05:0.95. Target $\ge 0.60$ [TARGET].
  • Landmark NME (landmark_nme): mean distance of the five points divided by the inter-ocular distance (points 0 and 1); box-only labels are left out. Target $\le 0.05$ [TARGET].
  • Measured values: not measured on the rebuilt labels yet.

metrics.csv uses a 17-column layout with Box_IoU, AP50, AP50_95 and Landmark_NME; the first run archives an older 28-column file as metrics_legacy.csv. Face detection has its own score scheme (face_box_weighted_targets_v1), so best scores saved under the old metrics are not reused. Metrics recorded before v16.9.207 / v16.9.208 are not comparable with new runs: the old map_* values were fixed multiples of a twice-squashed confidence score, and the rebuilt boxes cover about 0.31 to 0.57 of the old box area.

5. Challenges & Resilience Architecture

The current single-box design meets these challenges as follows:

  • Several or Microscopic Faces (< 16px): the head pools the whole feature map into one vector and predicts one box, so it has no per-location outputs for several faces or small faces. The published remedy, an FPN with anchors (optionally with Deformable Convolutions, DCN v2, on the low pyramid levels), is not implemented; adding a multi-scale anchor head is an open owner decision.
  • Extreme Profile Poses (Yaw > 60°): smooth L1 on normalized coordinates grows linearly for large errors, which bounds the gradient of a badly placed point. Landmarks whose ten values are all zero count as absent and are masked out of the loss.
  • Harsh Backlight & Glare: no dedicated mechanism; robustness is not yet measured.

6. Deployment Strategy & Production Acceleration

RetinaFace uses the training suite's release layout (registry file stem RetinaFace-Detection):

  • Production FP16 Engine (RetinaFace-Detection.onnx): FP16 graph with embedded weights for ONNX Runtime (DirectML, WebGPU). Latency is not yet measured; the earlier "sub-3ms [MEASURED]" claim had no benchmark behind it and is removed.
  • Research FP32 Export (RetinaFace-Detection_FP32.onnx + .onnx.data): FP32 model with separate binary weights sidecar, plus the self-contained PyTorch file RetinaFace-Detection.pt.
  • Lifecycle Governance: intra-epoch progress.pth checkpoints at most every 15 minutes (min_progress_interval_sec 900), latest.pth persistence, best.pth and per-metric vault checkpoints (vault_<metric>.pth).

8. Conclusion

RetinaFace with the MobileNetV3-Small backbone regresses one face box and five landmarks per image, the pre-flight orientation geometry for the LemGendary Face Suite. Its accuracy and latency are not yet measured, and a multi-scale anchor head for several or small faces is an open owner decision.
