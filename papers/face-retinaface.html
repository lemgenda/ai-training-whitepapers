RetinaFace: Multi-Scale Facial Landmark Localization

Single-Stage 5-Point Landmark Detection with Deformable Feature Pyramids

By Lem Treursic Category 08 FACE Version 16.9.16-STABLE Hardware: NVIDIA / MPS / XPU

1. Abstract

RetinaFace is a single-stage face localization network engineered for high-precision facial bounding box detection and 5-point landmark regression (left eye, right eye, nose tip, left mouth corner, right mouth corner). The LemGendary ecosystem supports two official authoritative backbones: MobileNetV1-0.25 for edge and real-time sub-3ms execution, and ResNet-50 for server-grade multi-scale Feature Pyramid Networks (FPN) and extreme crowd scenes. Paired with Deformable Convolutions (DCN v2) across pyramid levels \(P_2\) through \(P_5\), RetinaFace achieves verified detection stability under extreme head poses, harsh illumination, and microscopic face scales (< 16px).

1.1 What RetinaFace Does (In Plain English)

Imagine an intelligent autofocus system that finds every single human face in a bustling crowd scene, even if someone is turning away, standing in shadows, or wearing sunglasses:

  • Instant Face Localization: In under 3 milliseconds, RetinaFace scans an entire image, locating faces from full-frame portraits down to tiny faces only 16 pixels wide in stadium crowds.
  • 5-Point Landmark Pinpointing: For every detected face, it precisely marks 5 geometric anchors: the center of each eye, the tip of the nose, and the two corners of the mouth.
  • Essential Pre-Flight Alignment: These 5 landmarks allow downstream restoration models (such as CodeFormer) to automatically rotate, scale, and align tilted faces upright before applying facial retouching.

2. Feature Pyramid & Deformable Convolutions

Standard convolutions apply rigid sampling grids. RetinaFace applies Deformable Convolutions (DCN v2) across FPN levels \(P_2, P_3, P_4, P_5\) to adaptively deform receptive fields to match natural facial contours regardless of pitch, yaw, or roll.

3. Tri-Head Multi-Task Detection

Each anchor predicts three joint outputs: (1) Face classification probability \(p_i\), (2) Bounding box coordinate offsets \(t_i\), and (3) Five 2D facial landmark coordinates \(l_i\).

3.1 Multi-Task Objective & OHEM

$$\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{cls}}(p_i, p_i^*) + \lambda_1 p_i^* \text{SmoothL1}(t_i, t_i^*) + \lambda_2 p_i^* \text{SmoothL1}(l_i, l_i^*)$$

Online Hard Example Mining (OHEM) balances gradients during anchor classification.

4.1.1 Model description, purpose and usage

Single-stage face localization and 5-point landmark regression engine supporting both MobileNetV1-0.25 (Edge/Real-time) and ResNet-50 (High-Capacity FPN) backbones.

4.1.2 Model Info

  • Architecture: RetinaFace (MobileNetV1-0.25 & ResNet-50 with Deformable FPN)
  • Input Resolution: 640x640 (Multi-Scale Pyramid Testing)
  • Precision: ONNX FP32 / PyTorch FP32
  • Latency: Sub-3ms (MobileNet-0.25) / Sub-9ms (ResNet-50)

4.1.3 Manifold Info

  • Dataset: LemGendizedRetinaFaceMobileNetLarge
  • Total Samples: 128,400 annotated faces across WIDER Face
  • Primary Task: Simultaneous face bounding box classification and 5-point geometric landmark regression
