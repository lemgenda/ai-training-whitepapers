1. Abstract

This technical whitepaper details the architectural design, manifold engineering, loss formulations, and deployment specifications for the LemGendary Facial Vision Suite (Category 13). It coordinates CodeFormer (VQ-codebook blind face restoration), ParseNet (19-class anatomical face segmentation), and RetinaFace (MobileNetV1-0.25 multi-scale localization).

2. Visual Taxonomy: Facial Manifolds

Facial analysis models operate across three distinct data manifolds:

  • Blind Degradation Manifold (LemGendizedCodeFormerLarge): High-entropy synthetic blur, sensor noise, downsampling, and JPEG compression.
  • Anatomical Segmentation Manifold (LemGendizedParseNetLarge): Dense 19-class semantic masks resolving discrete facial regions.
  • Multi-Scale Localization Manifold (LemGendizedRetinaFaceMobileNetLarge): Bounding boxes and 5-point landmark coordinates across scales from 16px to 1024px.

3. Shared Foundations & Universal Acceleration

Memory bounds during training strictly satisfy:

$$\text{Mem}_{\text{total}} = \mathcal{O}(N \cdot H \cdot W \cdot C) \quad \text{vs} \quad \text{Mem}_{\text{peak}} = \mathcal{O}(b_{\text{chunk}} \cdot H \cdot W \cdot C)$$

Restoration quality is audited via the unified Scalar Restoration Quality Score:

$$\text{Quality Score} = \text{PSNR} + (\text{SSIM} \times 20) - (\text{LPIPS} \times 20)$$

4.1 LemGendary CodeFormer (Face Restoration)

Architecture: Transformer-Based VQ Codebook Lookup Network.

Status: Checkpoint Trained (LemGendaryCodeFormer-FaceRestoration.pt).

Target Manifold: LemGendizedCodeFormerLarge (40,000 high-resolution pairs, 512x512 resolution).

SOTA Targets: PSNR ≥ 30.5 dB, SSIM ≥ 0.930, LPIPS ≤ 0.080, FID ≤ 5.20.

4.2 LemGendary ParseNet (Face Parsing & Segmentation)

Architecture: Bilateral Semantic Segmentation Network (19 classes).

Status: Training In-Progress / Pre-Training Architectural Specification.

Target Manifold: LemGendizedParseNetLarge (512x512 resolution).

SOTA Targets: mIoU ≥ 0.860, Pixel Accuracy ≥ 0.965.

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{ParseNet}} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{CE}} + \lambda_{\text{lovasz}} \mathcal{L}_{\text{Lovasz}} + \lambda_{\text{edge}} \mathcal{L}_{\text{Boundary}}$$

4.3 LemGendary RetinaFace (Multi-Scale Face Detection)

Architecture: MobileNetV1-0.25 Feature Pyramid Network (FPN).

Status: Pre-Training Architectural Specification.

Target Manifold: LemGendizedRetinaFaceMobileNetLarge (640x640 resolution).

SOTA Targets: mAP Easy ≥ 0.915, Medium ≥ 0.890, Hard ≥ 0.750.

5. Challenges & Resilience Architecture

Pose variation, severe occlusions, and discrete codebook commitment losses are stabilized using random erasing augmentations, straight-through gradient estimators, and dense micro-anchor grids down to 4px.

6. Deployment Strategy

Models export to ONNX Opset 18 and WebGPU for deployment inside the LemGendary AI Studio GUI with < 350 MB total memory footprint.
